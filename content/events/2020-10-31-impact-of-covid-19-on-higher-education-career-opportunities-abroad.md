Rotract club of (Pune camp next gen, Pune camp Pioneer, SAOE) jointly initiated a project "Impact of Covid-19 on Higher Education & Career Opportunities Abroad" where an expert from Study smart Overseas education consultancy "Mr. Chetan Jain" provided necessary guidance and counselling sharing us the present status of COVID and its impact on the students who are willing to go abroad for their further studies, business/carrier or Job. We also enjoyed E-Scavenger Hunt where were given some riddles to solve within limited time.
