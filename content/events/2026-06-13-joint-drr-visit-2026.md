On 13 June 2026, the nine clubs of Zone XIII came together once again for the joint DRR Visit, continuing a tradition of collaboration for the third consecutive year. The visit was conducted in two parts: a documentation review by the RDC members, followed by presentations from each club highlighting their achievements throughout the Rotaract year. The session concluded with valuable remarks from DRR Rtr. Dinesh Gaire, District Secretary Rtr. Sairas Adhikari, ZRR 13 Rtr. Surakshya Niraula, as well the RDC members and guests present, followed by the presentation of tokens of appreciation in recognition of their guidance and support. Here's to another year of growth, accountability, and shared success!
