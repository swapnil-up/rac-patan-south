Fellowship with international clubs through a community driven project

Global Hand Washing Day Celebration was initiated by RAC Patan Durbar Square where various national and international clubs joined to teach proper hand washing to various ages of children. RAC Patan South along with RAC Patan Durbar Square, RAC Mahabouddha and RAC Sainbu Bhainsepati jointly organized this at Shree Shanti School where 40 students of different school participated. A documentary video was projected after which a practical demonstration of hand washing was shown by the resource person. Each participant was then handed over soaps so that they can adapt and teach others healthy habits.

- Duration: 10am to 12 noon
