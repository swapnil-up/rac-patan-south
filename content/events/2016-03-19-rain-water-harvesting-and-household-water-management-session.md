To know about rain water harvesting techniques and managing it in our households.

On March 19, 2016 RAC Patan South along with the clubs from Zone VIII conducted a one hour session on Rain Water Harvesting and Household Water Management. The session was conducted by the representatives of Best Pani who explained the vulnerable position of drinking water in Nepal and the consequences of not getting clean drinking water. They also provided us with the solution to check this problem by using Bio Sand Water Filters and Rain Water Harvesting Approaches. The session showed how small investments can help minimize water consumption and in the long term check the negative balance of water.

- Duration: 12:30 pm to 1:30 pm
