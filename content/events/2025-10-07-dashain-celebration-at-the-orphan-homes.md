RAC Patan South, jointly with RAC KUSOM, organized a dashain celebration program on 7th October, 2025 at The Orphan Homes, Lalitpur. The event included members putting dashain tika on the children, as well as cutting cake and the handover of small sets of stationery items. Seeing the smiles on the children's faces as they partook in the festivities was a happy reminder of why we as Rotaractors do what we do:)
