Voices were heard, perspectives were shared, and change was sparked! The energy in the room at NAARI: A Women’s History Month Symposium was truly inspiring. Thank you to everyone who joined, asked questions, and made this event so impactful! Together, we push for a more inclusive and equitable world. Conversations that matter. The NAARI symposium was a space for reflection, learning, and empowerment as we discussed the present and future of gender equality. From thought-provoking discussions with Aastha Subedi and Dipa Rai to engaging interactions with the audience, the event was a true success! Let’s keep the momentum going and work toward a more equal future! NAARI: A Women’s History Month Symposium 
On March 15th, we gathered at St. Xavier’s School, Jawalakhel, to engage in powerful conversations about gender equality and the evolving landscape of inclusivity across all domains. A huge thank you to our incredible panelists, Aastha Subedi and Dipa Rai, for their insightful discussions, and to our enthusiastic audience for their meaningful contributions! Special gratitude to our co-hosts, Rotaract Club of Bhadgaon and Rotaract Club of Lalitpur Midtown, for making this event a success!
