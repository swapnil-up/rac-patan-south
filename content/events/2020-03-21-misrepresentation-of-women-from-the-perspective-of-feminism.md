The Objectives of the project were:
* to clarify the concept of feminism
*to justify how the women have been misrepresented in the name of giving various rights
*remedies to uplift the status of women

On the occasion of the women's day which falls on march 8, we celebrated the day by conducing the discussion session on the topic 'Misrepresentation of women from the perspective of feminism'. The topic was facilitated by Miss. Priyanka Adhikari who is Bar candidate of 2020, currently working at reliance corporate service with previous experience at national and international media. This project had 40 participants who were participating from various clubs . The programme lasted for 1 hour and 30 minutes. The discussion session was successfully completed with an active interaction between the participants.
