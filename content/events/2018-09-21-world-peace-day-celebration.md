To promote world peace.
To extend fellowship among different clubs of different district and create a platform to know about each other.
To reflect how each of us call stand up for rights and peace everyday.
To unite every club under the same banner to signify our common wish for peace.

Project Detail:
We joined Rotaract Club of Patan and celebrated
The peace logo and Rotaract logos were made together using colored powder
After making the logos we lighted the candles and some oil lamps around Rani Pokhari in Patan Durbar Square.
We had the world peace day banner printed with logo of all the clubs involved all around Nepal
Project Outcomes:
Different clubs were present to celebrate and this extended fellowship amongst the clubs.
Non Rotarians also gathered around the place and celebrated with us.

- Duration: 3 Hours
- Rotaractors: 5
