To celebrate Tihar, the festival of lights, with Rotary family which includes Rotarians from Rotary Club of Patan South (RCPS), Rotaractors from Rotaract Club of Patan South (RACPS) and Interactors from Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel (ICSXJ) and Interact Club of Rato Bangala School (ICRBS).

Project Detail:
On the occasion of Tihar, RCPS, RACPS, ICSXJ and ICRBS gathered together to celebrate the festival of light with a fun-filled program “Deusi-Bhailo” There were dance performances from Rotaractors. Interactors, Rotaractors and Rotarians also indulged in singing performances during the event. The parent club, Rotary Club of Patan South, blessed RACPS, ICSXJ and ICRBS with a total fund of Rs. 13100.
Project Outcomes:
Celebration of Tihar with Rotary family. Fellowship among the Rotarians, Rotaractors and Interactors.

- Duration: 2 Hours
