Healthy habits begin with awareness! On 5 June, 2026, Rotaract Club of Patan South, in collaboration with Rotaract Club of KUSOM, Interact Club of St Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, and Rotary Club of Patan South, conducted 'Health, Hygiene, and You', an informative hygiene awareness program at Shree Motibinayak Lower Secondary School for students from Grades 1 to 8. The program featured two interactive sessions: the first focused on hand sanitation and personal hygiene, and the second on menstrual health and hygiene management. Through demonstrations and discussions, students learned the correct handwashing techniques and gained valuable knowledge about maintaining their health and wellbeing. By equipping young minds with essential life skills and health education, we take one step closer toward building healthier communities.
