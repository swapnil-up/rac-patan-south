Fellowship

To mark the Friendship Day which fell on 3rd August, Rotaract Club of Patan South along with Rotaract Club of Patan, Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree and Rotaract Club of Himalaya Patan organized Friendship Day Musical Program on 2nd August, 2014. There was huge participation from various Rotaract Clubs, host clubs and Rotaract District.

- Duration: 4 hours
