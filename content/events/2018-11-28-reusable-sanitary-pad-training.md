To acquaint participants about reusable sanitary pad
To provide training to the participants which shall enable them to make reusable cloth sanitary pad
To familiarize the participants on the effects of using chemical sanitary pad
To contribute towards eco-friendly living

Project Detail:
15 interactors (8 male, 7 female) of Interact club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel (ICSXJ) participated in the training facilitated by Rtr. Kripa Shakya of Rotaract Club of Patan South Pre-test questionnaire was distributed to the participants prior to the training The training focused on the need of eco-friendly living and comprised making of cloth sanitary pad. The facilitator also focused on the effects of using chemical sanitary pad The materials used for making a reusable cloth sanitary pad are as follows: Chart papers Scissors Stationery (chalk/sketch-pen, ruler) Fabrics (Flannel and Cotton) Needle and thread Snap fastener All the participants made their own reusable sanitary pad with guidance of the facilitator Post-test questionnaire was distributed to the participants in the end of the training
Project Outcomes:
Training was successfully conducted for 15 participants Almost all the participants (13 participants) was attending training for reusable sanitary pad for the first time Half of the participants had heard about reusable sanitary pad. Among them, most of had heard about it through the internet, whereas, the rest had heard it from newspaper sources or friends and family. They had the knowledge that the pad can be reused, it is homemade, eco-friendly and that it is made up of cloth. None of the participants knew anyone who uses reusable sanitary pad 10 of the participants were proud contributors towards an eco-friendly living. Some of the contributions made by the participants included carrying cloth bag, reusing everything at its best, not using petrol-powered vehicles at certain extent, stopped using plastic bags and cups and disposal of waste in a proper manner. Every participant had made one reusable sanitary pad in the end of the training Majority of the participants said that cloth sanitary pad is practical, whereas there were participants who disagreed as well The proficiency of the participants on making the pad varied from person to person. Almost all the participants could at least make a reusable sanitary pad fairly 4 out of 7 female participants said that they will use cloth sanitary pad over chemical sanitary pad, whereas, 11 of the participants said that they will recommend others to use reusable cloth sanitary pad over chemical sanitary pad. All the participants were satisfied with the training as per the post test results All the participants said that their expectations of the training was met. Feedback included suggestions to continue the training as it is very beneficial for the user as well as the environment.
