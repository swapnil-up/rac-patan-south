Build up public speaking skills

The 19th Public Speaking Session was the first session in RY 2016-17 a continuation of the forum for the third year. Rtr. Sneha Shakya was the Seargeant at Arms and Rtr. Tejan Shrestha was the Table Topics Master. The feature speaker and their respective topic were:
- Rtr. Kripa Shaky a from RC Patan South: "Our Grades dont define us"
- Rtr. Jayju Rajkarnikar from RC Kathmandu West: "Teej"
- Rtr. Gaurav Dongol from RC Patan: "Cooking"
- Rtr. Akshayaa Shrestha from RC Jawalakhel Manjushree: "My experience in Social Service"
In "Rotaract Public Speaking Sessions", we follow the basics of "Toastmasters International". There are three basic parts to our sessions: the prepared speeches, table topics, and evaluations. In the prepared speaking portion, we will have feature speakers who will give a prepared speech with the maximum time limit of 5 mins. "Table topics" is an an impromptu speaking with maximum time limit of 3 mins. For the evaluation we will have a Grammarian, an Ah-Counter, aTimer and a General Evaluator for each speaker.

- Duration: 10:30 am to 11:30 am
