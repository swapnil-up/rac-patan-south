Fellowship

Inter District Youth Exchange Program (IDYEP) 2018 was hosted by RID 3142 Mumbai from 1st August 2018 to 8th August 2018. Five Rotaractors were chosen from different clubs from RID 3292 Nepal and Bhutan for the youth exchange program. The team of five from RID 3292 Nepal and Bhutan consisted of: Rtr. Ashik Rajak, Vice-President, RaC Kathmandu Medical College, Rtr. Nirvisha Baidya, Secretary, RaC Patan South, Rtr. Nischal Shrestha, Immediate Past President, RaC Dhulikhel , Rtr. Susmita Adhikari, International Service Director, RaC Ktm North East and Rtr. Anant Agrahari, Head of Directors, RaC Butwal South.
The Clubs from RID 3142 that hosted them were:
1. Rotaract Club of Ambarnath East
2. Rotaract Club of Dombivli Midtown Youth
3. Rotaract Club of Ambarnath
4. Rotaract Club of Kalyan West
5. Rotaract Club of Link Town Airoli
6. Rotaract Club of Bharati Vidyapeeth Sunrise
7. Rotaract Club of Kalyan Central
8. Rotaract Club of Dombivli Suncity
9. Rotaract Club of Pune Alumni, RID 3131
10. Rotaract Club of Dombivli East
11. Rotaract Club of K.V. Pendharkar College
They also got to meet Rotaractors from Clubs from IDYE Chennai, RID 3232
1. Rotaract Club of Madras Chenna Patna
2. Rotaract Club of Blue Whales
3. Rotaract Club of Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering and Technology
4. Rotaract Club of Thangavelu Engineering College

- Duration: 8 Days
