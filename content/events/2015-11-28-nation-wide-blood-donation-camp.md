Collect blood

RAC Patan South joined hands and participated in the Nation Wide Blood Donation Camp. The clubs under Zone VIII conducted the camp at Jawalakhel Chowk on November 28, 2016. 66 pints of blood was collected from our venue and a total of 1369 pints was collected nation wide.

- Duration: 10am to 2 pm
