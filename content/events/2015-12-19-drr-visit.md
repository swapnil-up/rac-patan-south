Overall evaluation of the club

RAC Patan South successfully conducted the DRR Visit on December 19, 2015 at St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel. DRR Rtr. Samir Nepal, DS Rtr. Har Chandra Satyal, DRRE Rtr. Sushil Karki, DLT Rtr. Saugat Gautam and ZRR Rtr. Sneha Shakya were present during the meeting. The first hour was dedicated to the President, Secretary, Treasurer and President Elect. A general evaluation regarding the number of meetings, club memberships and procedures were done. In the second half, the DRR interacted with the club members. Avenue directors also presented on the various projects done under their avenue. The DRR also inducted five new members to the club. The suggestions and feedback provided by the District members were quite valuable and knowlegable. The club will certainly implement them in the days to come.

- Duration: 12pm to 2pm
