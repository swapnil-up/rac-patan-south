To enhance public speaking skills of Rotaractors.

Started on November 2014, Rotaract Public Speaking forum is continued Jointly with RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree, RAC Kathmandu West, RAC Patan and RAC Patan South. In this forum, a session in conducted on first Saturday of each English month. The forum has 4 featured speakers from each organizing club who speak for 5 minutes and 5-6 table topic speakers who speak for 2/3 minutes. Sessions are hosted by organizing clubs in cycle.

- Duration: 1 hour
- Rotaractors: 6
