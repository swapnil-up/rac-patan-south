To contribute towards Rotary's T.E.A.C.H. mission for Happy Schooling
To provide school with happy and creative learning environment
To bring zone 8 Rotaractors together for fellowship through service.

Project Detail:
All 8 Rotaract clubs from zone VIII were informed to participate in the zonal wall painting 2 weeks prior to the event.
Happy learning environment was the theme of wall painting.
The required materials for wall painting (paints and paint brushes) were provided to the Rotaractors who were in service
A total of 10 items were painted in the school premises which included paintings of Goddess Saraswati, kid-friendly pictures and informative writings.
Project Outcomes:
Moti Binayak School as a Happy school
Good learning environment
Fellowship among zone 8 Rotaractors.

- Duration: 5 hours
- Rotaractors: 41
