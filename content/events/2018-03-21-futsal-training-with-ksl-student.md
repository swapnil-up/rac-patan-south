• To train for the futsal tournament to be organised by RAC Chandragiri and RAC Mahabouddha • To fix the team ( both boys and girls ) for the participation in the tournament

Rotaract Club of Chandragiri and Rotaract Club of Mahabouddha had jointly organised the Zonal Futsal tournament on April 7, 2018. Rotaract Club of Patan South had to participate in the event. For that purpose, it was necessary to select a team for boys and girls from our club. On March 21 2018, the fellow rotaractors planned to play a practice match so as to select the team and train for the tournament. Rtr. Umesh Tharu arranged a practice match with the team of KSL students. The event started at 5 pm and ended at 6 pm.

- Duration: 1 hour
- Rotaractors: 8; Visitors: 4
