The Vidushe - Insights from Dr. Stuti Wagle event was a resounding success! With inspiring discussions about her career, lifestyle, and wellness journey, this session left everyone feeling motivated. We’re thrilled with the positive feedback this series has received, and we promise more inspiring sessions ahead! Our Vidushe series continues to inspire! The wonderful Dr. Stuti Wagle shared how she balances a thriving career as a Periodontist and Founder of The Tooth Clinic, while being a passionate advocate for wellness and a devoted mother. Her journey and insights were extraordinary. From advice about health to reflections on personal growth, everyone left with something valuable. Thank you, Dr. Wagle, for this thought-provoking session, and to all participants for making it a success! On 18th December, we were delighted to host the inspiring Dr. Stuti Wagle on Google Meet. A Periodontist, Founder of The Tooth Clinic, an advocate for health and wellness, and a mother, she shared her incredible journey of balancing personal and professional life. A big thank you to our co-hosts: Rotaract Club of People’s Dental College and Rotaract Club of Kantipur Dental College, for making this event possible.
