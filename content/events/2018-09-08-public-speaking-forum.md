To boost confidence in the speakers.
To encourage the participants to opine their views in a free and unrestrained manner.
To give space to creativity, and to create an atmosphere of learning from one another.
To help the speakers be aware of what could have been better in their respective presentations, consequently aiding them to be better speakers in the days to come.
To facilitate networking, consequently aiding in personal development of the club members.

Project Detail:
A total of three speakers from different clubs delivered their prepared speeches.
The commentators commented in the style and content of the speeches presented by the speakers.
The session was briefly followed by an informal session where some table topics were floored for everyone present therein, and the speakers voluntarily chose to speak in those topics.
Project Outcomes:
Kripa Shakya (RACPS) was announced as the winner of the day.
The club members got to increase fellowship among one another.
Creative ideas were heard, and the presenters got to know where they went a bit off and where and how they could have delivered themselves better.

- Duration: 1 Hour
- Rotaractors: 22; Visitors: 1
