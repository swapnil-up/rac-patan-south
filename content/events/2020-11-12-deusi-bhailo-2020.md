RAC Patan South, Interact Club of St Xavier's Jawalakel Rotary Club of Patan has been jointly conducting Deusi Bhailo programme every year. Due to the pandemic, the physical deusi bhailo programme was not possible to conduct physically so for this year the event was conducted virtually via zoom.
The rotaractors, interactors and Rotarians, all were involved in the meeting. Also, they gave some musical, dance, literature performance.

- Duration: 2 hrs
- Volunteering hours: 3
- Rotaractors: 21; Visitors: 3
