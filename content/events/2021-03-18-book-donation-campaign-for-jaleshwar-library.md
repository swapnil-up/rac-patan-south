CALL FOR BOOK DONATIONS------ 
Rotaract Club of Patan South in collaboration with Rotary Club of Patan South, Interact club of  St. Xaviers Jawalakhel and Scolar Kathmandu Hub is establishing a library with a youth club at Jaleswar-10, Mahottari. The library shall be accessible to all the village people at Mahottari and it is expected to benefit more than 150 families. We, as part of the Rotary family intend to promote Rotary Literacy Mission through this project. This library will be the first in the Jaleswor village area and it will engage children, youth and old aged people. Join us for this noble deed and donate books of all kinds - History, Politics, Literature, Fiction, Religion, Biography and others. Help us make a small change and build the foundation of education in this village and give them a ray of hope for a better Nepal. Thank you!
