Unity in service! Rotaract District 3292 joined hands with Zone 13 clubs for the 11th Late Rtr. Sachin Memorial Blood Donation. Hosted at the iconic Mangal Bazzar, Patan, this event not only collected 21 pints of blood but also celebrated the power of community and compassion. Rotaractors leading by example! The 11th Late Rtr. Sachin Memorial Blood Donation saw incredible participation from Rotaractors and generous donors alike. Together, we collected 21 pints of blood, proving that every pint counts in saving lives! A legacy of service lives on! Sachin Memorial Blood Donation was successfully held at Mangal Bazzar, Patan, on November 30, 2024. Organized by Rotaract District 3292 and Zone 13 clubs, the event honored the spirit of giving and brought Rotaractors together to make an impact.
