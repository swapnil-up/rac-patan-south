RACPS was thrilled to organize a CV Writing Workshop at St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel with Rotaract Club of Balaju, Rotaract Club of PDC, and Rotaract Club of KMC Duwakot as the co-host clubs.

The workshop was hosted by Samaya Khadka, CEO of Sharing Opportunities. His guidance and expertise made the session immensely productive, serving as a valuable learning experience for all participants.
