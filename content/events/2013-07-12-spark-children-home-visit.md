To give some skills to the children like dancing, singing, acting and to make them enjoy

Spark Children Home is a hostel for underprivileged children, located at Pulchowk, Lalitpur. This project was initiated the previous year. Rotaractors visit the hostel on weekends to teach the children dance, songs, music and help with their studies.
Every week around 4-5 Rotaractors visit the hostel. The main objective of this project is to teach the students skills that they do not get to learn in schools.
What we do is enjoy. We play with the kids, dance and sing.
Initiated in the previous Rota Year (12-13), this project shall continue for a few more months.

- Duration: 2-4 hours per visit
