Plant a Tree get Oxygen for free

1. We did a joint project with RAC Krishnaswamy College of Engineering and technology along with the participation of five different Rotaract clubs as mentioned above.
2. We also got the presence of Rotarians from RI district 2981 and the Rtd. Forest officers from the forestry department.
3. Firstly we inaugurated the program with the view and journey of Rotarian from RID 2981 and after one and half hours we started the live tree plantation of 5 different Rotract clubs.
4. Due to the outbreak of pandemic we Rtr. from RAC Patan south participated sending our pictures planting saplings at our own home same time as other clubs were going live.

- Duration: 3hr
- Volunteering hours: 3
- Rotaractors: 45; Rotarians: 1; Visitors: 2
