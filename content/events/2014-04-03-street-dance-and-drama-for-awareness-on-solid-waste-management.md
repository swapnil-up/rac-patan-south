Awareness on Solid Waste Management

A street drama and dance was organized by the club along with a group of youth dancers called "Eco- Flashmobbers". The dance was perfomed at the beginning which helped pull the crowd, after which the drama started. the drama focused on how the environment had been degrading due to mismanaged solid waste. It also focused on how the river Bagmati was getting polluted and also on the Bagmati Cleaning campaign going on. The drama also focused on how every person could contribute to reduce solid waste on his/her personal level - Reduce, Reuse, Recycle ; Compost Formation, its benefits and so on.
This project will also be continued in the environment month of June!

- Duration: 30 minutes
