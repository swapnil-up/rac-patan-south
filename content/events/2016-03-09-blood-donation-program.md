Collect blood

Rotaract Club of Patan South along with its parent club Rotary Club of Patan South along with twin club Rotaract Club of Patan, Rotary Club of Patan and Rotaract Club of Butwal South conducted a Blood Donation Camp on March 9, 2016. RAC Patan South, RC Patan South, RAC Patan, RC Patan conducted the camp at Jawalakhel Chowk, Lalitpur where we collected 41 pints of blood. RAC Butwal South conducted the camp at Blood Transfusion Office, Amarpath, Butwal where a total of 40 pints blood was collected. A total of 81 pints blood was collected at the two venues.

- Duration: 10am to 2pm
