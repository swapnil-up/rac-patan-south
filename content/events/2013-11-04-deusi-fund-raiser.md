Fellowship among members of the three clubs. Fund raiser for book drive and library setup in Jeetpur, Parsa

Two groups of people went around Lalitpur and Kathmandu on the 4th day of Tihar for Deusi Celebration. They visited Rotarians and fellow friends and collected a healthy sum, which shall be used for a library setup.

- Duration: 2 days (4th and 6th November)
