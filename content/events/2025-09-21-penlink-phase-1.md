Penlink — Phase 1
Phase 1 was conducted on the 21st of September 2025, with students of Grade 8 at Harisiddhi Secondary School, Lalitpur, Nepal. Rtr. Aakrity Rijal facilitated the session. After a short discussion with the students about their academic and extracurricular interests, Rtr. Aakrity wrote down some questions related to the discussion on the board, and asked students to write down their answers on a sheet. The list included questions such as what their current favorite subject in the curriculum was and why, and what subject they would like to see added. Answers ranged from ease in studying to competent and engaging teachers.
