The Blood Donation Program on December 21 was a testament to the power of community and collaboration. Thank you to everyone who donated and supported the event. Let’s keep the momentum going—donate blood whenever and wherever you can because every drop counts! The Blood Donation Program brought together individuals with one shared goal: saving lives. The collaboration with our parent club Rotary Club of Patan South made this event a huge success. Grateful to everyone who supported this cause! On December 21, 2024, we successfully hosted the Blood Donation Program in collaboration with the Rotary Club of Patan South and the Nepalese Society for Multidisciplinary Dentistry. It was an incredible opportunity to come together, serve the community, and make a difference. A heartfelt thank-you to everyone who participated!
