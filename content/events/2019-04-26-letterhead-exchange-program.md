Joint collaboration with other clubs for Letterhead Exchange with the club of Islamabad.
The main objective was to exchange letterhead to be able to build connections with clubs internationally

Project Detail:
Different Clubs came together at one place and conducted the letterhead exchange program The list of clubs who were a part of this program are: Rotaract Club of Kist, Rotaract Club of Yala, Rotaract Club of Patan South, Rotaract Club of Sainbu Bhaisepati, Rotaract Club of Chandragiri, Rotaract Club of Mahaboudha Clubs with whom the Letterhead exchange took place are: from 3142 District: Rotaract Club of Kalyan Central, Rotaract Club of Kalyan Central, Rotaract Club of Thane Downtown, Rotaract Club of Ulhasnagar Sapna Garden, Rotaract Club of Link Town, Airoli and Rotaract Club of Islamabad
Project Outcomes:
Different clubs came together and the entire program was very interactive and the members got exposed to individuals from other countries

- Duration: 1 Hour
