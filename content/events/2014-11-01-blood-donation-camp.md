To organize and participate in Nationwide Blood Donation Camp

Nationwide Blood Donation was organized on November 15, 2014 by 82 Clubs, in 22 different spots covering 15 cities. Rotaract Club of Patan South, along with Rotaract Clubs of Zone 8, R.I. District 3292, organized camp on Jawalakhel Chowk collecting 38 pints of blood. The overall collection from throughout the nation was over 1000 pints.

- Duration: 5 hours
