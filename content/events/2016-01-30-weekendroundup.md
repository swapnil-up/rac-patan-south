Information sharing on Event Management and Design Thinking

RAC Patan South conducted a session #WeekendRoundup with Swapnil Acharya and Amit Bajracharya on January 30, 2016 at Leapfrog Academy, Dillibazar. The first half of the session was taken by IPDRR Amit Bajracharya who is also the co founder of Namaste Kathmandu an event management company. He talked about the basics of event management by giving examples from his experience. The second half of the session was taken by Swapnil Acharya who is the co founder of Expresiv Studios. He talked about Design Thinking mentioning the basics about designing and design management. The session benefitted more than 40 participants.

- Duration: 11am to 12:30 am
