To discuss on past and future projects

After succcessfully completing two joint projects - World Peace Day Celebration and Blood Donation Camp, the relation between the two clubs had strengthened very much. To further the relationship and commemorate the success of the recent project, a joint meeting was held.
The agenda was about the future projects that could be held together. The President of RAC Patan also proposed that the two clubs proceed for Twin Club formation, which was accepted and agreed by all.

- Visitors: 3
