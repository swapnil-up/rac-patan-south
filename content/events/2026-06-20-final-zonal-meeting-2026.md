A fitting end to an incredible year with Zone 13. Held on 20 June 2026 at NSET, Bhaisepati, the final Zonal Meeting brought together eight of the nine clubs—including RACPS—for a joint collared meeting filled with reflection, appreciation, and celebration. The meeting featured a review of the Rotaract year, heartfelt remarks from club presidents, members, and ZRR Rtr. Surakshya Niraula, followed by the zonal handover to and announcement of the new zonal team by ZRR-Elect Rtr. Sabina Khanal. The event also included an award and recognition ceremony for the presidents by the ZRR, a felicitation, cake cutting, fellowship, and lunch. Here's to the memories we've made and the journey ahead!
