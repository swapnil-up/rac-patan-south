1) To understand better about Entrepreneurship in Nepal.
2) To introduce Entrepreneurship to club members for the
3) Next upcoming sessions.

The event was a joint event with the Interact Club of St. Xavier’s school and the Rotaract Club of Patan South on the first Saturday of the month of September. This was an introductory session for the club members of the Rotaract club of patan south and the interact club of St. Xavier’s school. The session had the objective to increase knowledge between the members of both clubs about Entrepreneurship. The presentation was presented by the Ayush Tamrakar and Utkarsh Manandhar, club members of Rotaract Club of Patan south who are also a student of Business and a well aware person in the business sector. This event also had the objective to increase fellowship between interact and Rotaract club as both representatives got to work together and learning from each other. The event had participation from both clubs with an attendance of 35 participants. This session was the prologue about entrepreneurship as it was the first phase of the beginning of the 3-month long event of Entrepreneurship तीन दृष्टिकोण. In the later phases of the 3-month long event, the main objective is to understand the problems and solutions of Entrepreneurship in Nepal. The different perceptions would be provided from the Startups, Mediocre Business, and Star Businessman from the country. This aims to provide help to all the future entrepreneurs of Nepal and make others familiar with the concept of business in Nepal as well. The session was 45 minutes long which ended with a Question/ Answer session for the presenters. The session fulfilled the objective as all the participants were able to have a basic knowledge of Entrepreneurship.
