Stronger Together! Introducing our Co-Host Club President 
Partnering with us as a co-host club leader for the landmark 19th Rotaract District Assembly, 2nd PS Meet, and DLTS Recognition Ceremony! we can't wait to see you all on June 27, 2026, at BICC, Butwal
Let’s make history together! On 27 June 2026, RAC Patan South attended the 19th Rotaract District Assembly, District Leadership Training Seminar (DLTS), Award and Recognition Ceremony, and 2nd PS Meet at BICC, Butwal—the final district event of RY 2025-26. The day celebrated the collective efforts of clubs and individuals across the district. RAC Patan South was honored with multiple recognitions for its projects throughout the year, including the Innovative International Service Award. President Rtr. Aakrity Rijal, received the DRR Choice Award, while District Secretary Rtr. Sairas Adhikari and ZRR13 Rtr. Surakshya Niraula were recognized as Best Supportive RDC Members. As the tenure draws to a close, these recognitions stand as a testament to a year of service, dedication, and teamwork. To ending on a high note and looking ahead to another impactful year!
