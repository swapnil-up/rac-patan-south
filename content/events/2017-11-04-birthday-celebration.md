To celebrate birthdays of every member until November
To increase bonding and interaction between club members

A surprise cake was brought in, after a general meeting which was scheduled ended. Every club members whose birthdays were before November celebrated their birthdays by cutting the cake. Also, there was a game in between where a hat was there and the person holding the hat should name three different members. As there were a lot of new members in the club this game helped to at least remember the names of club members. Adding to the celebration, there were handmade birthday cards for each and every members who attended the event. The cards were made by Rtr.Nirvisha Baidya , Rtr. Tejan Shrestha, Rtr. Grisma Sinya and Rtr. Sajan Ratna Shakya.

- Duration: About 2 hours
- Rotaractors: 23; Visitors: 1
