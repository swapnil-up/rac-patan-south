Greetings! Rotaract Club of Patan South plans to celebrate International Literacy Week starting from Sep 8th, 2020 to Sep 15th, 2020. Rotaractors/participants need to share a post/story on Facebook/Instagram mentioning their book of the week. Post Criterias
Option 1 - Post a picture of the book and write about your experience/feelings regarding the book. Option 2 - Post a video reciting your favorite paragraph from the book sharing a view of the book cover. Mandatory
Tag us in your Facebook/Instagram postRotaract Club of Patan South using hashtag
