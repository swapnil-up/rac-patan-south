RAC Patan South is organizing the first session of ViduShe 3.0--- a flagship RAC Patan South event with esteemed guest Dr Madhu Dixit Devkota who is the executive chairperson of the Upendra Devkota Memorial National Institute of Neurological and Allied Sciences in Bansbari, Kathmandu. She also serves as an associate professor contributing to the medical field and healthcare education in Nepal. Join us to get exclusive insights regarding the field of Neurology and guidance from Dr Madhu Dixit Devkota and her story to success. 15/08/58 marked a truly special milestone for our club — our very first event, VIDUSHE. We were honored to have Prof. Dr. Madhu Dixit Devkota Ma’am, executive chairperson of Upendra Devkota Memorial Notional Institute of Neurological and Allied Sciences (commonly known as Neuro Hospital, Bansbari), with us for an insightful and inspiring session that encouraged us to look at mental health with greater awareness, empathy, and understanding. A heartfelt thank you to Upendra Devkota Memorial National Institute of Neurological and Allied Sciences for your valuable support and for helping us make this event possible. We are equally grateful to our wonderful co-hosts, Rotaract Club of Sainbu-Bhaisepati, Rotaract Club of Changu and Rotaract Club of Kathmandu West, for joining us in making our first event even more meaningful. Our first event, our first step~and the beginning of many more impactful initiatives to come. Here’s to learning, serving, and creating change together.
