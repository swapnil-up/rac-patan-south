The Last General Meeting of RY 2025–26 brought together familiar faces and new beginnings at Doko Deli Restaurant, Jhamsikhel. As President Rtr. The meeting also marked the induction of our newest members into the RACPS and Rotaract family. Here's to carrying the legacy forward, together.
