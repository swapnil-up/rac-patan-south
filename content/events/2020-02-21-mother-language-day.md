-To promote linguistic and cultural diversity and encourage multilingualism.

Rotract Club of Ambarnath initiated an idea to celebrate International Day of Mother language. We along with 36 different clubs from 33 different districts and 16 different countries participated in the project. We were given the sentence "I am a proud Rotaractor" to convert it into our mother tongue within 5 seconds.

- Duration: 1 hr
- Volunteering hours: 1
- Rotaractors: 55
