On May 22, 2021, Rotaract Club of Patan South conducted a Twin Fellowship program with Rotaract Club of Patan who have been our twin since the past 8 years. We started the program with a icebreaker and continued with soothing musical performances from Rtr. Kiran GC, Rtr. Prashant Tandon and Rtr. Sushant Thapa. We had a very interesting game where everyone was divided into groups. We also had the oresence of past presidents of both the clubs Rtr. Sneha Shakya, Rtr. Sajal Shrestha, Rtr. Peshal Bhattarai, Rtr. Sajan Ratna Shakya and Rtr. Ayush Suwal whi shared their experiences of becoming a twin and working together throughout these years. The program was fun and everyone had fun connecting with each other.
