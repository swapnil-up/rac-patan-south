-to teach the grade 6 students more about menstrual hygiene and making of the reusable sanitary pad
-open discussion between young boys and girls
-awaring them about the environmental impact of making the switch from store bought sanitary pads (plastic pollution)

-initiated with sole purpose to spread awareness to people of all age, all caste, all gender about menstrual hygiene and teaching how to make a reusable sanitary pad
-initiated because the plastic pollution worldwide is increasing with every passing second and that making a switch to reusable sanitary pad is proved to be better economically, environmentally and with regards to better health as well
