Did you know? Hair loss during cancer treatment significantly impacts self-esteem and emotional well-being for many patients. To address this, "उपहार" was organized in collaboration with the Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, spreading hope and restoring confidence for those battling cancer. Together, let’s create a stronger, more compassionate community. A warm gift, a new beginning. Through "उपहार," in collaboration with the Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, this beautifully crafted wig brought comfort and confidence to a cancer warrior. While their face remains unseen, their joy shines through every strand of this heartfelt gift. Together, we are spreading love, one wig at a time! "उपहार: A Warm Gift for Cancer Battling Patients" 
This meaningful event, held in collaboration with the Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, aimed to provide wigs to cancer warriors, symbolizing hope, strength, and love during their fight. Every wig shared tells a story of compassion, and every effort reflects a commitment to making a difference.
