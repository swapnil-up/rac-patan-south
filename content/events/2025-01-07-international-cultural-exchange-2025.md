Our members truly made the International Cultural Exchange 2025 special! From actively engaging in discussions to sharing their own traditions, everyone’s enthusiasm lit up the virtual room. The fun icebreaker activity was the perfect start, bringing smiles and breaking down barriers. Together, we created a space of understanding, respect, and joy. A big thank you to every participant for making this event an unforgettable experience! Let’s continue building bridges and celebrating what makes us unique. At the International Cultural Exchange 2025, we delved into the richness of global cultures through heartfelt presentations by our amazing members. From traditions and food to festivals and history, each story was a window into a world of uniqueness and beauty. Thank you to all the presenters for sharing a piece of your culture with us and enriching our perspectives. Here’s to embracing and celebrating diversity together! International Cultural Exchange 2025 
On 7th January 2025, we collaborated with the Rotaract Club of University of Colombo Faculty of Management and Finance to bring together a vibrant celebration of cultures from around the globe! This online event was an incredible platform for members to share their traditions, learn about others, and foster meaningful global connections. A huge thank you to everyone who joined us on this enriching journey. Together, we proved that diversity is what truly unites us!
