Celebration of friendship day within Rotaract clubs.
To meet young energetic people having similar interests
To know more people and increase your friend circle
To increase interaction between different clubs

The event started around 5 P.M. There were nearly 70 people. The host clubs had planned some entertaining games like bingo and friendship quiz, and live music performances. Snacks had also been arranged. A total amount of NRs 5725 was raised from the participants of the event.

- Duration: 4-5 hours
- Rotaractors: 9; Visitors: 15
