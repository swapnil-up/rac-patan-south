As part of our ongoing commitment to fostering professional development, the Rotaract Club of Patan South is delighted to announce an event tailored specifically to enhance professional skills: the CV Writing Training. This event aimed to equip our members with practical skills and strategies to strengthen their professional profiles, ultimately empowering them to excel in their chosen fields. Collaborating with St. Xavier’s School, Jawalakhel, we curated a comprehensive training session on CV writing, a crucial aspect of career advancement. Our esteemed guest speaker for this event is Dr. Khagendra Acharya, Associate Professor of English at Kathmandu University. Dr. Acharya brings a wealth of expertise in trauma literature, social identity, and critical theories, with a specialization in English language teaching and research methodology. His impressive credentials include a PhD from Kathmandu University and notable publication records in various academic journals. Moreover, Dr. Acharya has been honored with several prestigious scholarships and grants, underscoring his commitment to academic excellence and professional development. The event was a great success. Thanks to everyone for joining in the event.
