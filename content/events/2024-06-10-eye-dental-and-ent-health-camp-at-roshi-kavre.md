Rotaract Club of Patan South along with Rotary Club of Patan South, Interact Club of SXJ, Jawalakhel and RAC KUSOM organized an Eye, Dental and ENT health camp at Roshi ward no.3 Kavre in collaboration with BP Eye Foundation. It was a successful event with more than 250 people. We distributed Spectacles, medicine and provided dental service. The response from the ward was very overwhelming and amazing.
