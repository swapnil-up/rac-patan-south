1) Donate the previously collected clothes to the SPCA Animal Shelter
2) Spend time with the animals at the shelter
3) Discuss with the people at SPCA Nepal regarding possible future activities

1) The clothes were collected from the GAA Hall where the donation box had been completely filled.
2) The President Rtr. Ayush Suwal and Community Service director Rtr. Mayukh Shrestha collected the box and donated the box to the SPCA Animal Shelter. The tattered clothes and blankets that we had collected for animals in SPCA NEPAL shelter were later converted into cushions, mattresses, and clothes to keep the street animals under their care warm during winter.
3) The members of SPCA Nepal were welcoming and animals in the shelter were excited to see us during the visit.
4) The dogs in the shelter were playful, however, we were unable to interact with the cows and cats as they were further into the compound of the shelter.
5) After spending about an hour with the animals, the participants had to head home due to changing weather.

- Duration: 2 hrs
