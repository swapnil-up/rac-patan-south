The exposure of the chat screenshots of the Instagram group - "Bois Locker Room" was shocking and mind-shaking for many of us. Thus the club planned to host a discussion session regarding this sensitive issue, focusing on the following topics:
- Recap regarding the "Bois Locker Room situation"
- Importance of speaking up about such issues / Platforms for speaking up
- Rape Culture
- Sexual frustration among teens
- General Discussion
The featured speakers of the session: (Per speaker 10-15 mins)
1) Kripa Shakya
2) Mayukh Shrestha
3) Anupam Bista
4) Shreya Parajuli
5) Sushil Sharma

1. The discussion session started at 6 pm sharp where we waited about 10 mins for everyone to join the online session.
2. Rtr. Sushil Sharma from RAC East Coast Chennai elaborated on "The Boys Locker Room" situation.
3. Rtr. Kripa Shakya Focused on the importance of speaking up and acting upon subjects such as the effects of victim-blaming, the impact of economic class upon rape crimes, and the effects that normalization of objectification of females by the media has upon the general public.
4. Rtr. Anupam Bista and Shreya Parajuli elaborated on the cyber laws present in Nepal that Deals with cybercrimes related to such scenarios and the potential development of Cyber laws in Nepal.
5. Rtr. Mayukh Shrestha elaborated upon the experiences that boys in Nepal have to go through that can potentially lead to regular frustration or sexual frustration, and the importance of proper conversation between parents, teachers, and children about their changing bodies and minds.
6. The discussion portion of the session proved to be the most fruitful as the participants engaged themselves in providing different perspectives to each other.
