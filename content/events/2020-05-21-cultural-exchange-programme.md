This programme was conducted for members to get acquitted with different culture and its practices that we have in our south Asian region.

On the occasion of the world day for cultural diversity Rotaract Club of Patan south along with Rotaract club of Ambarnath and Rotaract club of East Coast Chennai hosted the cultural exchange programme. South Asia has always been the hub of diverse culture and its practices. The practice of one place could completely differ with another with its unique practices.
This programme was conducted for members to get acquitted with different culture and its practices that we have in our south Asian region. Three clubs, one from Nepal and two from india came together to conduct this programme.
The presenters from RAC Patan south were Rtr Rashie Agrawal, Rtr Bibek Kumar Thakur and Rtr Lumanti Manandhar. Rtr Rashie presented about mardwari community and its practices as a whole, Rtr Bibek kumar Thakur presented about the maithali culture from terai region of Nepal and Rtr. Lumanti Manandhar presented about the the tradition of ‘Belbeha’ which is a practice performed by people from Newar community of Nepal.
Presenters from RAC Ambarnath Rtr Vishal Rathor presented about the Gujrati Culture and Aishwarya warke presented about the Marathi culture. And the presenter form RC East coast Chennai, Rtr. Sushil and Rtr Dhanush jointly presented about the tamil cluture that is practiced of south india.
To conclude the programme was successfully completed with active participation and interaction from the members. The participants claimed the session to be fruitful and informative.
