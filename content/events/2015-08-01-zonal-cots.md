Training to Club Officials and members

RAC Patan South along with RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree from Zone 8 and RAC Patan and RAC Himalaya Patan from Zone 7 hosted the Joint Zonal COTS. The training program was held on August 1, 2015 in the premises of Kathmandu College of Management. The training started at 12:30 pm with the opening session facilitated by IPDRR Rtr. Amit Bajracharya and DLT Rtr. Saugat Gautam. They shared the basics and structures of Rotaract.
The second session started at 1:15 pm where parallel avenue wise training was given to the President/Secretery (Rtr. Amit Bajracharya), Treasurer (Rtr. Utsav Mani Kharel), avenue directors (Rtr. Hari Satyal, Rtr. Binayak Dahal, Rtr. Suraj Khadka, Rtr. Sushil Karki), and General Members (Rtr. Saugat Gautam). The program ended at 3:30 pm which was followed by refreshment.
