Fellowship and team building

On April 9, 2016, RAC Patan South along with parent club RC Patan South and inner wheel IC St. Xavier's School organized a friendly futsal fellowship program in Maidan Futsal, Old Baneshwor. Two teams were formed each from Rotaract and Interact and the remaining players were chosen from amongst the Rotarians. A total of three games were played during which members of each of got to interact with members of the other clubs. The program also developed the spirit of sportsmanship and team.

- Duration: 9 am to 12 noon
