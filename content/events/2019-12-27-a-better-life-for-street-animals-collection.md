1) Collect clothes for Street animals so that tehy can be warm during the winter
2) The collected clothes were to be donated to SPCA Nepal where they would transform them into blankets, clothes and mats for rescued street animals in the SPCA shelter

1) A collection box was placed in the GAA hall for 3 weeks where the clothes were to be donated.
2) Resources used:
- 1 box
- 2 Chart papers
- 4 Highlighters and 1 marker
3) Fellow rotaractors were prompted to donated any access clothes, cushions or mats that could be useful for the shelter animals
4) A full box full of clothes that weighed about 12 kg were collected.

- Duration: 3 weeks
