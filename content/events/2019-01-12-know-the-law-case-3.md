To impart knowledge regarding the work, employment and labour laws in Nepal.
To facilitate healthy discussion regarding the real life job scenarios and to educate about the real life fundamentals regarding the said topic.

Project Detail:
The event was organized by Rotaract club of Patan South, jointly with Rotaract Club of Mount Everest, and Rotaract club of Kathmandu Metropolis.
A speaker from legal background gave a presentation regarding the said laws.
Various inquiries were raised from the audience, and a significant portion of important issues were discussed apart from the keynote speech itself.
Project Outcomes:
Important legal provisions regarding labour and employment were discussed.
A healthy discussion regarding the real life job scenarios did occur.
The real life fundamentals regarding any employment were discussed.

- Duration: 3 Hours
