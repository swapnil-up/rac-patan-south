Greetings Rotaractors! Rotaract Club of Patan South is hosting the most recent Public Speaking Session with the theme of “The Festival Story“ along with RAC Kathmandu West, RAC Kasthamandap, RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree, and RAC Patan. Festivals are vital parts of our lives as they are the carriers of peace and joy in the communities. They unite people and help everyone embrace their culture and religion. Since we live in such a diverse country, why not share about our festivals, the celebrations in your communities, your interesting customs, and your traditions. Let’s all come together and learn the stories of festivals of different communities present in Nepal. Session details: Date: November 28, Saturday at 11 am
