To increase fellowship within club members

The project was initiated with the aim of cultivating a sense of fellowship during the holiday season. This particular event was special for us because it meant that a lot of us were meeting for the first time since the pandemic had begun. We randomly assigned a person to gift another person with a budget restriction of Rs. 500. Those of us who could make it, met at RedMud cafe at Jhamsikhel to exchange the gifts. We opened these gifts on Christmas day at a virtual meeting. The virtual event was also complete with songs, laughter, and jokes to celebrate the spirit of Christmas.

- Duration: 1 week
- Volunteering hours: 1
