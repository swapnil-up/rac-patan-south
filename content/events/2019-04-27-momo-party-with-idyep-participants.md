Hosting of IDYEP Participants
Fellowship

Project Detail:
Club members gathered at Rtr. Nirvisha Baidya's Residence at 11am to welcome participants of the IDYEP participants from Mumbai Rotaract District 3142. Once all the members were gathered they introduced themselves to the IDYEP participants, everyone proceeded to making momos and teaching the Mumbaikars how to make momos.
Project Outcomes:
Fellowship amongst the club members and IDYEP participants.
