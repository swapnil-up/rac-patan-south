To know about the Do's and Don'ts of preparing and delivering Interview

On January 16, RAC Patan South along with RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree, RAC Patan and RAC Kathmandu West conducted a session on Job Interview. The session was fascilitated by Rtr. Bibek Thakur, Secretary of RAC Patan South. The one hour session consisted of the Do's and Don'ts while preparing and delivering an interview. The facilitator also provided information of the different types of interviews and how to tackle such interviews. Some hacks to prepare for an interview were also given.

- Duration: 10am to 11am
