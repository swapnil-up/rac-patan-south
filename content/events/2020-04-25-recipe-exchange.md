To exchange recipes between the two clubs to share our culinary diversity.

After the completion of the joint meeting, it was time for Recipe exchange. RAC Patan South provided links to two video recipes (Wo and Gwaramari) whereas RAC East Coast Chennai (RID3232) shared four recipe videos (Spinach Corn Sandwich, Beef Parotta, Prawn Fry and Egg Masala). Members were excited to get new recipes to try out and agreed to send photos when they tried the recipes.
