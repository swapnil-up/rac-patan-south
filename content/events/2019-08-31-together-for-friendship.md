To improve fellowship with members and other clubs

Jigrees at 8 degrees was a fellowship event organised by 8 Degrees in Jhamsikhel, Lalitpur jointly by Rotaract Club of Patan South, Rotaract Club of Himalaya Patan, Rotaract Club of Patan and Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree to celebrate the month of Friendship. On registering, the rotaractors got to choose colored bands to determine their relationship status (red, yellow and green). Rotaractors gathered to groove to the music by talented rotaractors while dedicating notes and songs to their friends. The highlight of the event was Bingo, a definite crowd pleaser. The event helped increase fellowship among the rotatactors.

- Duration: 5 hrs
- Rotaractors: 100
