With great excitement, we announce that Rotaract Club of Patan South is jointly hosting a Blood Donation Program with King's College. Event details:
Date: 22nd July 2022 (Friday)
Time: 9 A.M. Location: King's College, Bijuli Bazar, Kathmandu
Join us in this noble humanitarian cause as blood donors to save lives. DONATE BLOOD SAVE LIFE 
Blood donation is the most precious gift! Rotaract Club of Patan South successfully completed our Blood Donation Campaign in King's College Nepal
Thank you to the members of King's College Social and Socialization Club for helping to manage the program with such smoothness. Special thank you to all our Rotarians for the support and guidance
