To help children learn basic information about periods at early age, benefits of using reusable sanitary pads and thereby avoiding pollution

39 students of Grade 5 and 6 participated in the training. First, RAC Kasthamandap conducted Menstrual hygiene awareness program to aware students about the importance of menstural hygiene and safe practices during mensturation. The students were divided in a group and were given the fabrics (flannel and cotton canvas).They were directed to cut the cloth into pieces and to sew it to make reusable sanitary pad. All the students showed their interest in making reusable sanitary napkins.

- Duration: 1 hr
- Rotaractors: 7; Visitors: 39
