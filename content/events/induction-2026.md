We are pleased to announce the induction ceremony for the Rotaract year 2026-27. This event marks the official beginning of our term and welcomes new members into the Rotaract family.

The ceremony will include:
- Oath-taking for new board members
- Introduction of the year's projects and goals
- Fellowship and networking
- Light refreshments

All members, prospective members, and guests are welcome to attend.
