- To teach the women about menstrual hygiene and the making of reusable cloth sanitary pad - To help them become aware of the rise in plastic pollution, health problems created by store-bought sanitary pads - To help them know how it differs and is advantageous economically, environmentally and a healthy alternative

The project was solely initiated to help tackle plastic pollution as well as create a platform where both men and women learn freely about periods. It was also so that women face lesser health problems during periods, additionally, it helps them save money in the long run.
In this session, 22 Rotaractors of Kathmandu West were given the training to make the DIY Sanitary Pad.

- Duration: 3 hours
- Rotaractors: 22; Visitors: 1
