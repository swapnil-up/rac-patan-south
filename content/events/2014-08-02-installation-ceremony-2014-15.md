2nd Installation Ceremony of Rotaract Club of Patan South was held on 2nd August, 2014 at Degaa Resto Longe. The event was marked by handover of the Charter Certificate, Gong, Gavel and President's Collar by outgoing President Rtr. Sneha Shakya to incoming President Rtr. Sambid Ghimire. The installation of new board for Rota Year 2014-15 was also done. The event was graced by presence of Rtn. Shiva Bahadur Nepali Pradhan, Past President of Rotary Club of Patan South, Rtr. Amit Bajracharya (District Rotaract Representative), District Officials and dignitaries from fellow Rotaract Clubs. We wish President Rtr. Sambid Ghimire and his team the best wishes for the new Rota Year.:D
