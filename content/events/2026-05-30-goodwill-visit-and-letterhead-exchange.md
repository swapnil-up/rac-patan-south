Goodwill Visit & Letterhead Exchange 
On May 30 2026, the Rotaract Club of Patan South had the privilege of conducting a goodwill visit to the Rotaract Club of Himalaya Patan. The visit provided a wonderful opportunity to strengthen fellowship, exchange ideas, and celebrate the spirit of Rotaract. As a symbol of collaboration and mutual support, we also had a letterhead exchange with both Rotaract Club of Patan Durbar Square and the Rotaract Club of Himalaya Patan. Here's to building stronger connections, fostering lasting friendships, and creating a greater impact together through service above self.
