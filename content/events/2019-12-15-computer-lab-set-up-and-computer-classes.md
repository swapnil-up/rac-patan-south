1) Collect computers and distribute them to Motibinyak
2) Provide classes to the children at Motibinayak School for digital literacy and implement the TEACH mission.

1) The club collected 15 computer sets from NIBL Bank that were to be replaced by newer models
2) The computer sets were brought into the school, however, it was necessary for the installation of proper wiring in the computer lab.
2) The club organized an electrician for the wiring and later on set up 10 computer sets while 5 computer sets were placed as backups.
3) President Rtr. Ayush Suwal conducted a series of computer classes for the children.
