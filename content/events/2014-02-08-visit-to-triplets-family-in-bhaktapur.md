To support the family

A little further form the main city of Bhaktapur, there is a poor family who recently gave birth to triplet daughters. The couple already have a elder daughter of 5 years.
During the Blood Camp, one of the nurses present Mrs. Sharmila informed was about the family and requested us to support the family. So, the two clubs paid a visit to the family, taking along some food materials for the mother, warm clothes for the children and some financial aid.
The health of the triplets was not good at birth but slowly they were improving. After spending some time with them and talking about the children and the mother, we took leave. The family was very grateful.

- Duration: 1 hour
