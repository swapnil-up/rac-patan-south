Last Saturday, RAC Patan South organized our first community clean-up drive around Patan Durbar Square.

Over 20 members showed up with gloves, bags, and plenty of enthusiasm. Together we collected over 50 bags of waste and planted 15 saplings along the roadside.

Thank you to everyone who participated and to the local community for their support!
