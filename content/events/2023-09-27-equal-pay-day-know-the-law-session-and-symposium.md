In observance of Equal Pay Day, Professional Development Committee of RACPS conducted a significant event, featuring:
1. Know the Law Session: Discovered insights into equal pay and related laws as we set the stage for the symposium. 2. RACPS Equal Pay Symposium: Following the 'Know the Law' Session, we explored the gender pay gap. Our central topic is "Is the Gender Pay Gap a Result of Systemic Bias or Individual Choices?" The discussion aimed to address the existence of the Gender Pay Gap and whether it is primarily driven by gender bias or differences in skills and experience. Additionally, we provided certificates of participation and high tea to all attendees. We are delighted to share a formal recap of our recent Equal Pay event, marking the second event for the year. The event was executed with enthusiasm, and we extend our gratitude to all who contributed to its success.
