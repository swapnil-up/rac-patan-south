A monumental chapter for RAC Patan South at the SEARIC Summit Nepal! After months of anticipation, planning, and tireless dedication, the highly awaited South Asian Regional Rotaract Conference came to life at Hotel Pokhara Grande from December 19-21. We are immensely proud to share that our very own IPP Rtr. Surakshya Niraula, PP Rtr. Sairas Adhikari, and PP Rtr. Sneha Shakya stepped up to serve on the core organizing team alongside DRR Rtr. Dinesh Gaire. From coordinating logistics to hosting on the main stage, their leadership was instrumental in hosting Rotaractors from all across South East Asia. Thank you to everyone who traveled across borders to make this summit a historic success. The cross-cultural ideas sparked here will resonate for a long time to come!
