“You find peace not by rearranging the circumstances of your life, but by realizing who you are at the deepest level.”—Eckhart Tolle
Rotaract Club of Patan South in collaboration with Rotaract Club of Yala along with with Rotaract Club of Thane Premium RID 3142, is conducting a webinar, “शान्त मन, बबाल जीवन” to KNOW THE INNER CALMNESS & PEACE. Although people have started to take this pandemic as the NEW NORMAL, it gets overwhelming time and again to adapt the change. Therefore, we bring you this 3-days webinar with an objective to help you STAY CALM, UNDERSTAND YOUR CALMNESS and ACTIVATE THE CALMNESS IN YOU while relieving your stress and other mental health issues that you might be facing and you didn't know. By this, we urge you to join us for:
Day 1 STAY CALM
Date: 8th October 2020, Thursday
Facilitator: Dr. Dushyant Bhadlikar, MBBS and MD, Trainer of Therapy for Psychological Trauma called EMDR. Objective: Introduction to types of mental health issues like Anxiety, Depression, etc. Further, the session will facilitate on how we face mental health issues in our lives without realizing it and why is it necessary to be talking openly about it.
