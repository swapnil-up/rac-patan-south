A Morning of Care and Togetherness 
On October 20, 2026, the occasion of Nawami, Rotaract Club of Patan South, in collaboration with the Rotary Club of Patan South and the Rotaract Club of KUSOM, visited Manavsewa Ashram, Nagdaha. The visit was filled with warmth and meaningful moments as we spent quality time with the elderly residents, shared conversations, and created moments of togetherness. We then distributed the essential items and served breakfast to the residents as a small gesture of love, care, and support. This Nawami, we were reminded that the true essence of celebration lies in sharing kindness, spreading happiness, and being present for one another.
