Discussion on Gender and Applicable Laws of Nepal

Know the Law was conducted with discussion of gender and applicable laws of Nepal. Rtr. Dikchya Raut and Rtr. Bibek Kumar Thakur presented on the topic with example of various cases and was followed by interactive discussion between the speakers and participants. The event was very informative and received good reviews from the participants.

- Rotaractors: 7
