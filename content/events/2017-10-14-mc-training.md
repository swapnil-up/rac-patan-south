Training students of art of emceeing

Rtr. Abhinab Mahat (RAC Mount Everest), Itr. Shailesh KC (Interact Club of St. Xavier's Jawalakhel), Rtr. Anupam Bista, Rtr. Dipmala Chapagain and Rtr. Bibek Kumar Thakur facilitated participants from grade 9 and 10 of Amar Kanti English Boarding School, Lele on various topics including introduction to the art of Emceeing, scope of being a good MC, formats and decorum, art of speaking and technical aspects of Emceeing. The Principal and Vice-principal of the school thanked the rotaractors and Interactors for bringing this project. A very positive reaction was given to this project. The school also requested for other programmes.

- Duration: 1 hours
