Fellowship among the clubs

On January 23, RAC Patan South along with RAC Patan went to a Hike to Chapakharka, a hill above Godavari. All the members gathered at Lagankhel and headed towards Godavari from where we would start our hike. After reaching the destination all the members sat down and gave a detailed introduction of themselves. The hike promoted fellowship within and between the members of the two clubs. After staying for about two hours we started moving downhill.

- Duration: 8am to 4pm
