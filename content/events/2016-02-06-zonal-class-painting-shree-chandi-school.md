Paint the classroom and strengthen the Zone

As a Zonal Level project, RAC Patan South along with all the other seven clubs from Zone VIII participated in the Class Painting Project at Shree Chandi School, Chakupat. The project was carried on for two consecutive Saturdays where we painted two classrooms and the corridors. The project also helped to bulid fellowship among the clubs in the zone.

- Duration: 12pm onwards
