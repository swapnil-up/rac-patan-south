A huge round of applause to our amazing poets and musicians who graced the stage at Poetry Saanjh! Your words and melodies filled the night with magic and left us all inspired. To celebrate their incredible talent, we had exclusive gift hampers for all our participants – because art deserves to be appreciated. Thank you for making this evening unforgettable! Let’s keep spreading the love for poetry and music. Poetry Saanjh: An evening where words find their wings. On 30th December 2024, we transformed Asado Pub & Grill into a haven for poetry and music lovers. From heartfelt verses to the soulful tunes of the sarangi and violin, every moment celebrated the beauty of art and connection. A heartfelt thank you to our incredible sponsors for supporting this vision and making the night extra special! Here’s to more evenings where creativity thrives. Here’s a glimpse of the magic that unfolded at Asado Pub & Grill on 30th December 2024. From soul-touching poetry to enchanting melodies of the sarangi and violin, the night was a perfect blend of emotions, art, and connection. The laughter, the claps, the conversations, and even those quiet moments where words lingered in the air – every second was priceless. To our participants, sponsors, and audience – THANK YOU for being a part of this poetic journey. Let’s do it all over again soon!
