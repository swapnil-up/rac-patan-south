Peace and prosperity in the world

World Peace Day is celebrated on September 21. RAC Patan south along with RAC Patan, RAC Yala, RAC Mahaboudha, RAC Lalitpur, RAC Patan Durbar Square, RAC Bhaktapur, RAC Kathmandu Midtown, RAC Dhulikhel, RAC Pokhara, RAC Biratnagar, RAC Dharan, RAC Butwal, RAC Birtanagar Downtown and RAC Ambarnath from International District 3140, India did a lighting program in their respective venues. RAC Patan South was assigned to one of the temples that broke down in the April 2015 earthquake.
On the same day, a Joint International Bulletin was released. The club wishes peace and prosperity to the families who lost their loved ones and for the peace and welfare of our nation.

- Duration: 5pm to 8pm
