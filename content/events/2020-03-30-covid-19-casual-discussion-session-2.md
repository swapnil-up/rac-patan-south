1) Discuss the potential impact that the lockdowns have on Nepalese as well as the world economy.
2) Discuss the importance of road structure and traffic maintenance during the pandemic
3) Tackle misinformation regarding COVID-19

1) The Session started off with the guest Speaker Babin Manandhar describing the importance of directing traffic properly and decreasing the chances of accumulation of masses during rush hours
2) The rest of the participants were interested in the impact of road traffic during semi-lockdowns and the application of a flexible road traffic system.
3) Rtr. Utshav Bajracharya provided his perspective regarding the impact that codiv_19 may have upon the economy. He compared the current scenario with the 2015 earthquake as well as previous events where lockdowns were seen in Nepal. He was optimistic that the recovery of the economy would be quick once the proper vaccines were produced.
4) The participants then were involved in a discussion session where they discussed about the present condition of society and how people were affected because of the virus,
6) Everyone was happy with the session in the end and were thankful for the featured speakers of the session as the participants felt that they were able to learn a lot from the session.
