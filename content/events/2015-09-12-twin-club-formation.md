Renew the tenure of Twin Club

On September 12, 2015, RAC Patan South and RAC Patan renewed their tenure of Twin Clubs up till June 31, 2016. The two clubs went through the signing two years ago which promoted mutual benefit for each other. To continue the same relationship, the club extended the date for one more year. The MOU signing was done in a Joint Meeting with the presence of ZRR Zone 7- Rtr. Sudeep Shakya and District Publication Officer Rtr. Summit Bajracharya.

- Duration: 9am to 10:30am
