Introducing, Penlink — Reading and Connecting Across Borders! A joint venture from RAC Patan South and RAC University of Colombo, Faculty of Management and Finance, as part of our twinship, this project aims to promote reading habits among school students, as well as understand their dreams, goals, aspirations and expectations from the education system of their country. Penlink — Phase 2
The second phase of Penlink was conducted on the 27th of January, 2026 at Harisiddhi Secondary School, Lalitpur, Nepal—the same school where the first phase was conducted. To encourage reading habits, students of Grade 7 were asked to read a couple paragraphs out loud to the class, from English-language books as well as Nepali-language ones. The students also wrote letters to their Sri Lankan counterparts, about their hobbies, interests, school, etc.
