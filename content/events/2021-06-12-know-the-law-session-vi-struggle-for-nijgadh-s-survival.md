Anju Mandal, aged 28 had suffered from Blood Cancer in 2018 and she fought the disease and got better. But now the cancer cells have reappeared and she is getting treatment at Nepal Cancer Hospital, Lalitpur. She needs to go through Chemotherapy and Bone Marrow Transplant treatment. The treatment costs more than 25 lakh. And this time her family is facing difficulties to arrange the treatment costs. To support the treatment of Miss Mandal RAC Patan South, RAC Kathmandu Metropolis and RAC Mount Everest is organizing Know the Law Session VI on the Topic "Struggle For Nijgadh's Survival". Advocate Sanjay Adhikari from Pro Public Nepal will be the speaker for this session. Register here: 
Note: ALL THE AMOUNT COLLECTED FROM THIS EVENT WILL BE DIRECTLY DONATED FOR THE TREATMENT OF ANJU MANDAL.
