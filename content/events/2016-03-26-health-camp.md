Conduct a free health camp in an area that does not have proper medical facilities.

RAC Patan South and parent club Rotary Club of Patan South conduted a Health Camp on March 26, 2016 at Valley Public School, Lamatar. 262 patients were thoroughly checked at the Health Camp. The camp served four departments: Dental, Gynecology, Pediatric and General Checkup. The patients were also provided with the necessary medication for their problems. A team of eight Rotarians, five Rotaractors, an Interactor and sixteen doctors visited the school and conducted the camp for the day.

- Duration: 9:30 am to 3:30 pm
