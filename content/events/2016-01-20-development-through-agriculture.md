Provide agricultural tools to mechanize agricultural practices

With more than 70% of the people involved directly or indirectly in agriculture, the project focuses on providing modern and automatic agricultural tools and equipments to the people of Jaleshwar-10, Mahottari. All the works are done manually which takes a lot of time and labor. Due to this the parents are not being able to provide good education to children because they are not able to work in the farm without the help of their children. For the first phase, we provided them with Cono Weeder, Rice Threesher, Dry line Weeder, Linner, and Spray. The implementation of this project is expected to increase production in the farm saving Rs. 3000 per family per year. 60 families will be benefitted from the project.

- Duration: Two days
