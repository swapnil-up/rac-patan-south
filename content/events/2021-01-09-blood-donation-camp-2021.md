To address the shortage of blood brought by noble corona virus throughout the country Rotary club of Patan South, Rotaract club of Patan South and Nepalese Society of Multidisciplinary Dentistry is organizing Blood Donation Event on Poush 25 2077 (Jan 09, 2021), Saturday at Jawalakhel Chowk. We would like to invite you all, to join hands with us in this noble humanitarian cause as Blood donor. Organized by:
1) Rotaray Club of Patan south
2)Rotaract Club of Patan south
3) Nepalese society of Multidisciplinary Dentistry
Supported by: 
1) RID 3292 Zone 8
2) Nepal Cycle Society 
3) Nepal Running Club 
4) A step towards change
Event Details:
Time: 11-2pm
Location: Jawalakhel chowk (infront of Salt Trading Office)
Date: 25th Poush, 2077 (9th Jan 2021) The previous year 2020 has definitely taught us some valuable lessons. One of the most vital one is that we can all get through anything when we join hands and be there for each other. To address the current shortage of blood due to the Covid-19 crisis, we are organizing a Blood Donation Campaign. All safety measures will be taken care of during the campaign keeping in concern about the Corona Virus spread. Join us in this noble humanitarian cause as blood donors to save lives. Blood donation is the most precious gift! Organized by:
1) Rotary Club of Patan South
2) Rotaract Club of Patan South
3) Nepalese society of Multidisciplinary Dentistry
4) Scolar Kathmandu
Supported by: 
1) RID 3292 Zone 8
2) Nepal Cycle Society
3) Nepal Dynamic Running Club
4) A Step: Towards Change - एक पाइला: परिवर्तनतर्फ
Event Details:
Time: 11-2pm
Location: Jawalakhel chowk (infront of Salt Trading Office)
Date: 25th Poush, 2077 (9th Jan 2021) “Whatever you do will be insignificant, but it is very important that you do it.” 
With the help of everyone, our blood donation campaign was successfully executed. Thank you to all our donors for their contribution, big thank you to Rotary Club of Patan South, Nepalese Society of Multidisciplinary Dentistry and Scolar Kathmandu Hub, our supporters RID 3292 Zone 8, Nepal Dynamic Running Club, A Step Towards Change and Nepal Cycle Society. Thank you all!
