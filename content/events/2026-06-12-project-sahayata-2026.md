Project Sahayata: Because a little help goes a long way 
On June 12, 2026, RAC Patan South successfully conducted the second edition of Project Sahayata at Shree Motibinayak Lower Secondary School, Dholahiti. Originally initiated by Rtr. Surakshya Niraula during her tenure, this project continues its mission of supporting students through educational resources. This year, 150 students, from kindergarten to Grade 8, each received a school bag and a stationery kit, helping equip them with the tools they need for their learning journey. Here's to carrying forward a legacy of service and investing in brighter futures—one student at a time.
