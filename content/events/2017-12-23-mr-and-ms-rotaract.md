To explore hidden talents of Rotaractors.

Kathmandu Auditions
Date: 27th October 2017
Venue: Unicampus Global, Putalisadak
Members Present: 7
Finals
Date: 23rd December, 2017
Venue: Army Officers Club, Sundhara
Club members present: 11
The first ever Mr. and Ms. Rotaract in RI District 3292 was a grand success. The final event held on December 23, 2017 witnessed the announcement of the first Mr. and Ms. Rotaract in RID 3292! The Top 18 contestants presented themselves for the competition and the Top 3 were selected through various rounds. The Top 18 were selected through numerous rounds of auditions, out of a pool of 200+ applicants. The Top 18 contestants also attended a Closed Training Camp before the main event, with various training sessions, interactive sessions, tasks and more. The main event was successfully concluded in the presence of 650+ Rotarians, Rotaractors, Interactors and guests.
There was tremendous support and encouragement from the District Governor Rtn. Sanjay Giri, District Secretary Rtn. Prafulla Man Singh Pradhan and Rotary District Team; Acting DRR Rtr. Suraj Poudel and Rotaract District Committee. Regional Hosts - RAC Butwal, RAC Dharan Ghopa, RAC Narayangarh, RAC Newroad Pokhara, RAC Tilottama Rupandehi and Audition Judges helped the organizing team in finding the best of participants from different parts of the country. Finale Judges and Closed Camp Evaluators Rtn. Srijana Bhurtel, Rtr. Amit Bajracharya, Rtr. Saugat Gautam and Rtr. Sameep Pokhrel supported the organizing team by investing their tme time and dedication in mentoring and evaluating our participants in the Closed Camp.

- Duration: 3 hours
