A hike that mattered. Along the beautiful Budhanilkantha trail, we picked up what others left behind — leaving the path cleaner than we found it. Big shoutout to the crew for showing up strong! Let’s keep hiking with heart and purpose. Hiking with purpose! On March 22, we took the trail to Bishnudwar with a mission — to clean as we climbed. From early morning meetups to handing over collected trash to park officers, it was a day of action and awareness. Grateful for everyone who showed up to hike for a cleaner tomorrow! We hiked. We cleaned. We made a difference. In the serene trails of Shivapuri National Park, RAC Patan South joined hands with Rotaract District 3292’s Cleaning Campaign — proving that even small efforts can create lasting impact.
