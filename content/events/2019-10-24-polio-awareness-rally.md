Raise awareness regarding Polio

1) We participated in Polio Awareness Rally on Oct 24, 2019 marking the World Polio Day.
2) The rotractors that attended the event were able to be involved in a rally with a cause to keep Kathmandu Polio free.
3) The participants walked from Bhrikutimandap to Bhadrakali –Tripureswor- Thapathali- Kopundol- Pulchowk - Party World Banquet as part of the project plan.
