Late Rtr. Santosh Memorial Nationwide Rota Quiz 2018-19 was organized by Rotaract Club of Chandragiri jointly with Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree for Zone 8. It happened on 29th December 2018 at K&K International College, Baneshwor. The winners of the zonal level were to compete in the final level at the "11th Rotaract District Conference" which is going to be organized by Rotaract Club of Parbat.
Three of RAC Patan South's teams participated in the Late Rtr. Santosh Memorial Nationwide Rota Zonal Quiz. One of the teams consisting of Rtr. Ruby Shakya, Rtr. Tejan Shrestha and Rtr. Nirvisha Baidya secured first place from Zone 8.
