This event was a collaborative event, including RAC Patan South, in partnership with RAC Kasthamandap, Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree, RAC Kathmandu West, and RAC Patan. We proudly present the first public speaking event of Rota Year 23/24, held on September 16th. This event featured five speakers from each participating club, engaging in an intellectually stimulating discussion on the topic, "Is the use of AI in education beneficial?" We are delighted to share a formal recap of our recent Public Speaking event, marking the commencement of our joint project for the year. The event was executed with enthusiasm, and we extend our gratitude to all who contributed to its success.
