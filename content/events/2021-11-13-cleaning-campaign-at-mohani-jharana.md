On November 13, 2021, Rotaract Club of Patan South held a Cleaning Campaign to Clean Mohani Jharana. We collected over a hundred water bottles and other plastic materials scattered around the waterfall and hope to raise awareness regarding managing your own waste and discourage people from littering.
