He is the owner of Fitbox and Cage gym. He will be talking about different types of problems and solutions that a person has to face during startup. Please Directly message us for meeting link. Mr Denim Krishna Shrestha - an entrepreneur and a fitness expert is the founder of Fitbox Nepal and Cage Gym. Fitbox Nepal is formed on the motive to inspire people and lead them to a healthy lifestyle with nutritious food and a fitness gym. In the modern day where people are busy of their daily life, many feel the need for guidance and support on their fitness journey and Fitbox is doing just the right thing. Cage Gym located in Gwarko has also been helping people transform their body health. He also looks forward to increase his field of business in the future. DM us to register for the event!
