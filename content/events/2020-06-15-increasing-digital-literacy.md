Increasing Digital Literacy
We set forth to forth to setup a computer lab in a government school in the valley so that we could help teach the students in the school how to use basic computing tasks. We got the required computers through the generous donation of NIBL bank Ltd. and with the financial help of Rotary Club of Patan South we setup the required electrical wiring for the lab. Once we completed the setup Rtr. Ayush Suwal taught the students how to use the computers with classes every Thursday and Friday. The classes is scheduled to resume once the Covid-19 crisis passes.
