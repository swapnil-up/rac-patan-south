To create awareness on Menstrual Hygience and Sanitation

Menstruation is big issue in the Nepalese Society, considered bad and unholy. We see that females are ill treated during such times, often leading to unhealthy and torturous activities.
It was also seen in a survey that most girls in Government Schools drop out in class 7,8 or 9 due to problems of menstruation (either because of lack of facilities at school, or due to pain, family pressure and not being able to manage properly). Keeping this in mind we started a awareness campaign, starting from Adarsha Kanya School.
We worked with Prof. Ishwor Amatya, from Pulchowk Campus. He introduced us to a trainer from Korea. This was her first attempt at a Nepali School and also would be the last one as she was leaving in a month. So our members decided to continue the project after she left.
The awareness was about what actually menstruation meant and how it was to be understood. The trainer also talked about how the members of our society misinterpret it and that it should be removed. She also taught the students how they could manage during ther menstruation days. She also taught some exercises which would help reduce pain. Lastly, she taught the attendees about the use of sanitary napkins and how they could also prepare home made napkins. The students also given a few napkins as samples and were asked to talk about this topic at home to their sisters, mothers and friends.

- Duration: 1.5 hours
