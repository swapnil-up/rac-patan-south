To set up a library in Nepal Rastraya Secondary School, Siyarahi Sathwal, Parsa, Jeetpur

The club members worked for more than two months to collect books, organizing book drives in various school and colleges - Kathmandu College of Management, Nepal College of Management, St. Xavier's Jawalakhel, Rato Bangala School, Mount Valley School. The KCM Social Welfare Club and Interact Club of Mount Valley School contributed a lot in this project. The three host clubs also purchased books from the funds collected during Deusi. More than 2,000 books were collected. The members worked collecting books from various places, mending the damaged books, sorting according to level and subjects and packing them for delivery.
The sponsoring Club - Rotary Club of Patan South took the books to the project site. Mega Health camp and Libraru Setup was done by the sponsoring club on 14th December.

- Duration: 2 months
