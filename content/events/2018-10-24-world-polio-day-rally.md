To make the community and the cluse aware about Polio
even though Nepal is Polio free our aim is to make people aware about polio till it gets to zero world wide.

Project Detail:
Nepal National PolioPlus Committee organized the rally in Kathmandu.
the rally started from bhrikutimandap and ended in kupondole.
all participants were given a shirt, cap and small flags which had information about Polio and the need to eradicate it.
Project Outcomes:
Different clubs were part of the rally.
with the mass and so life Musical instruments being played during the rally we were able to gain attention of good mass and make them aware about Polio.

- Duration: 2 Hours
- Rotaractors: 4
