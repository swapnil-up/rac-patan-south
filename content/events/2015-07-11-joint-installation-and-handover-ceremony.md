Installation of the incoming Board of Directors for Rota Year 2015-16

RAC Patan South along with RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree, RAC Kopundol and RAC Sainbu Bhainsepati did a Joint Installation and Handover Ceremony with the presence of DRR Rtr. Samir Nepal as the Chief Guest. The program started with a brief presentation of Rota Year 2014-15. A handover ceremony was done which was followed by the installation of the new Board Members. The joint installation ceremony sowed friendship among the four clubs.

- Duration: 3pm to 5pm
