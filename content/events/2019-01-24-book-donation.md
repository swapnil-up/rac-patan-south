Celebrate International Day of Education by donating storybooks and Novels to New Horizon School as they did not have any storybooks in their library.

Project Detail:
Rtr. Lumanti Manandhar coordinated with the school and managed the time of the principal so that we could hand over the books. We donated 100 books including both Nepali and English. We had also collected tons of course book but we donated this to schools in Jhapa.
Project Outcomes:
The children of New Horizon School now don't have to limit themselves to just course books. The school wanted to donate extra course books they had to the needed schools if any. This built a good connection with the school as well.
