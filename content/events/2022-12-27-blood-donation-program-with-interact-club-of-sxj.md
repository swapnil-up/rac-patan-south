Donating blood can help save many lives. There are still many cases where lack of blood caused death of a person. To avoid such consequences, RAC Patan South and Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel are conducting a blood donation program on Dec 27, 2022. We encourage everyone to be a part of this campaign and donate blood.
