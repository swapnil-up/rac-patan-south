Bringing together everyone who enjoy writing, reciting and listening to poetry.
Celebrating the magic of words in the month of love.
Fellowship among Rotaractors.

Project Detail:
The session was combined with Rotaract Public Speaking organized by RAC Patan.
A total of three slam poets from different clubs presented their spoken words poetry.
A small thank you-gift was presented to each of the poets.
Project Outcomes:
The club members got to increase fellowship among one another.
Admirers of poetry got to share the same platform, while expressing their love for poetry in different ways.

- Duration: 1 Hour
- Rotaractors: 17
