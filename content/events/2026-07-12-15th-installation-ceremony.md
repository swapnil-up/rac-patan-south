A look back at the 15th Installation Ceremony of the Rotaract Club of Patan South. Held on 12 July 2026 at Pulchowk Library Hall, the ceremony marked the official handover of leadership from Rtr. Aakrity Rijal to Rtr. Yukie Maharjan for the Rotary Year 2026–27. We were honoured to celebrate this milestone in the presence of our respected Past Presidents, distinguished guests, members, and our sponsoring club, Rotary Club of Patan South 
Thank you to everyone who joined us as we celebrated our legacy and stepped into a new year of service, fellowship, and growth. Here's to making a meaningful impact together! After our 15th Installation Ceremony, we shared the remaining food from the event with individuals in need around Pashupatinath. As we begin Rotary Year 2026–27, we're reminded that service is often found in the simplest of actions. We hope to carry that spirit with us throughout the year. The day concluded with a small act of sharing. Service Above Self.
