1) To increase public speaking ability of Rotaractors
2) For fellowship among fellow Rotaractors
3) To give continuity to the culture of Rotaract Public Speaking Culture.
4) To improve Rotaractors and Participants professional caliber

Rotaract Public Sepaking Session has been one of the events of Rotaract Club of Patan South, RAC Patan, RAC Jawalakhel Manjushree and RAC Patan West which was started quite a while ago now. This event was mainly started with the purpose of improving public speaking among Rotractors as this skill has been very crucial for our professional life.
In our session of Public Speaking we followed the Toastmasters format of public speaking and followed it so that Participants attending the session become aware about the public speaking format and experience it.
The theme of the meeting was "New Year Resolution", here the featured speakers had their speeches prepared and spoke about topics related to the theme. Other participants also participated in table topics. In table topics random topics were given and participants spoke instantly.
So overall event was a success as Public speaking event had not been continuing smoothly for some time and we made sure proper rules and regulations were followed and professionalism was maintained. There was a sense of fellowship in the air and laughter with humorous speeches as well.

- Duration: 1.5 hrs
- Rotaractors: 4; Visitors: 2
