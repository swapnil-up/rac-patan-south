Rotaract Gives Back! This project is to establish a definitive Community Service Project based on our Rotaract Network. RAC Patan South along with our supporting clubs have come together to provide Heaters, Water kettles, Hand warmers and Blankets for children in 3 Orphanages. We have also collected blankets for animals and collected Rs. 7,500 for the treatment of dogs. Supporting Clubs:
Interact Club of St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, Rotaract Club of Patan, Rotaract Club of Lalitpur, Rotaract Club of Yala, Rotaract Club of Mahabouddha, Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree, Rotaract Club of Patan Durbar Square, Rotaract Club of Himalaya Patan, Rotaract Club of Kantipur, Rotaract Club of KTM Metropolis, Rotaract Club of Kathmandu West
