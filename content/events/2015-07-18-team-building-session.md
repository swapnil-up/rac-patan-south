Team building among the members and between the members of other clubs.

Rotaract is a member driven club where all the activities are planned and coordinated by the members. Roles and responsibilities might vary but the purpose and direction of all the members are the same. Here comes the importance of teams and considering its importance, Rotaract Club of Jawalakhel Manjushree and Rotaract Club of Patan South organized a Team Building Session on July 18, 2015 at Future High School, Jawalakhel. The session was facilitated by District Leadership Trainer Rtr. Saugat Gautam and Rtr. Sameep Pokharel.
Nine activities were conducted in spontaneous teams each activity reflecting the principles of teams. Leadership, proper communication, coordination, conflict management and trust are the elements that reflect the proper functioning of teams. Along with this practical learning, a good rapport was built between the members of the two clubs.

- Duration: 12 noon to 4 pm
