A day to remember! The KARUNA event at the HIV/AIDS Center for Girls was all about breaking the silence and embracing menstrual health with confidence. Watching the girls engage, learn, and grow was truly inspiring. Let’s continue to normalize these conversations! Smiles, strength, and sisterhood! KARUNA was more than just a session — it was a safe space to ask questions, share stories, and embrace menstrual health without shame. Let’s keep the conversation going and ensure no girl feels alone in her journey. KARUNA: Menstrual Health & Hygiene 
On March 4th, 2025, we came together at the HIV/AIDS Center for Girls in Godavari to break barriers and start important conversations about menstrual health. From learning about different period products to tackling the stigma around menstruation — it was a day of empowerment and awareness!
