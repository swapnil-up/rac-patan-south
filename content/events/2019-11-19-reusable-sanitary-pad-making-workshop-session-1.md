To make every girl who menstruate aware that their small choice from plastic loaded disposable pad to reusable pad makes a huge difference to their health and environment.

An awareness session was conducted in which all associated topics related to menstruation were explained to adolescent school girls, like what is menstruation, calculation of the period , avoiding the traditional methods of using dirty cloths and promoting use of sanitary napkins and reusable home-made sanitary pads.
After the session, students were very happy to get new information about menstruation. Also, they were happy to learn the patterns to make reusable sanitary pad.

- Duration: 1 hr
