To contribute towards Rotary's T.E.A.C.H. mission for Child Development To provide an opportunity to the students who are in need of a platform To emphasize on overall growth of students (focusing on practical activities) To provide an opportunity of best practice sharing among 2 schools

Project Detail:
More than 200 science projects were displayed during Science Exhibition at St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel Three students from Moti Binayak School were given an opportunity to showcase the models of the following items at the Science Exhibition: Vacuum cleaner Water pump Dozer Dumping Truck The students were accompanied by 3 teachers from Moti Binayak School The students demonstrated their models to the visitors during the Science Fair with proper explanation.
Project Outcomes:
Contribution towards Rotary Literacy Mission: Child Development Exposure to the students of Moti Binayak School regarding the following aspects: Presentation skills Speech delivery Explanation Best practice sharing between St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel students and Moti Binayak School The students got a chance to learn more on Science projects during the exhibition

- Duration: 2 Hours
- Visitors: 6
