Promote friendship among the clubs.

RAC Patan South did a Goodwill Visit to the General Meeting of RAC Sainbu Bhainsepati on July 25, 2015. The meeting was conducted in their regular meeting venue – NSET, Bhainsepati. The two clubs exchanged letter heads and cards expressing their mutual interest to enrich the fellowship that the future clubs have developed over the years.

- Duration: 9am to 10:30am
