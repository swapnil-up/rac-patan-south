Mind and Me is an initiative aimed at mental wellbeing and mental health awareness among the youth. Phase I of this series was held on 21 March. The session was facilitated by Chanchal Dhakal, an Australia-based registered clinical counsellor who is also the face behind manobal.therapy. With professional experience in youth mental health and counselling, she led an interactive session aimed at helping young people understand mental health, recognize early signs of concern, and know when to seek support.
