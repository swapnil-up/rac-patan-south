To handover laptop, projector, stationary materials, MIDAS Softwares
To fix it in Sundar Prabi of Borlang-6, Gorkha
To train the teachers about the use of softwares

Students of Smith College including our Charter Vice President Rtr. Liza Maharjan organized several fund raisers organized on campus as noted below:
They made momos and sold them on campus
They organized a cultural performance and dinner night to collect funds
They set up a henna tattoo and face-painting stall during family weekend
They collected Online donations
The donations were made by students, faculty as well as the extended Smith community. The donations were collected online via GoFundMe page, as well as setting up donation booths on campus. The donated amount was given to Rotaract Club of Patan South to reach Sundar Pra bi at Simaltar, Gorkha. Rotaract Club of Patan South co-ordinated with Rotaract Club of Gorkha and collected stationary materials like copies, sharpener, eraser and pencils for students. Midas software for students upto grade 3 was installed and taken to Sundar Pra Bi.
March 10: The school administration Welcomed us at 4p.m. We had snacks together . We started the technical setup and called all the villagers and concerned stakeholders at school in open field at 6.00 pm. The official program started at 6.30 p.m. The school administration, Head of various committees in School and representatives from Ward Office addressed the mass. The laptop and projector was opened by the eldest member of the community.
Administration committee Member Mr. Bikash Kumal, Parents Committee Member Mr. Kamal Shrestha, Local Government Member Mr. Jiwan Kumal, Head Master Mr. Krishna Rana, Project facilitator Mr. Ramesh Kumal and President of Rotaract Club of Patan South Rtr. Bibek Kumar Thakur addressed the mass.
Mar 11: The collected stationary items and Laptop and Projector was handed over to the school administration by President of Rotaract Club of Patan South, Rtr Bibek Kumar Thakur and President of Rotaract Club of Gorkha, Rtr. Buddhi Jogi. Both the clubs promised to take the project to great heights.
March 12: We met the students of the school and left Gorkha.

- Duration: 3 days
- Rotaractors: 4; Visitors: 47
