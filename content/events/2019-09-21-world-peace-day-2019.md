1. To spread the message of peace in our community
2. To highlight the harmful impacts of climate change under the theme "Climate Action For Peace"

Rotaract Club of Patan marked the "The International Day of Peace" with the theme "Climate Action For Peace".
Rotaract Club of Patan South participated in the same. On the theme "Climate Action for Peace" this year, we tried to highlight the dangers of climate change, causes of such changes and its impact in the environment. We tried to attract the attention of the bystanders my putting on the handmade posters. People came and enquired. We felt glad that people cared. The event concluded with lighting diyos around the Maharani Pokhari of Patan Durbar Square.

- Duration: 2 hours
