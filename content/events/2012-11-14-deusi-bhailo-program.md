On the occasion of Tihar, members of RAC Patan South along with few memebers from the Interact Club of St. Xavier’s organized a Deusi-bhailo fund raising event on the 14th of November. The members visited the houses of many Rotarians and performed musical song and dances along with the traditional deusi-bhailo song. Apart from the enjoyable experience for the members, the club was successful in raising a total amount of Rs. 21030. It was a very fun and memorable. We sang, danced, rapped, chased by a drunk man, and collected tons of Money for our Club:)
