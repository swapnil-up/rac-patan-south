• To identify Rotaractors with qualities of being ambassadors of change
• To give a platform to talented Rotaractors to come out of the crowd and showcase
their talents
• To develop a team of young people from different parts of the country to lead the
movement to bring innovation and sustainability in Rotaract Clubs.
• To enhance the brand image of Rotaract among general public as an organization
that fosters leadership growth and personal development of youth

Rotaract Club of Patan South along with 6 other Rotaract Clubs hosted Mr. and Ms. Rotaract Season 2 at Nepal Academy Hall, Kamaladi, Kathmandu. The auditions were held on January 26 in 5 major cities Bhairahawa, Chitwan, Itahari, Kathmandu, and Pokhara of Nepal.
Total of 77 candidates were selected through the first round of auditions. These candidates were given a video and writing task where they made a video with the theme 'Why should young people join Rotaract?' and a project proposal supporting Rotary Nepal Literacy Mission.
From the second round of selection, 36 candidates were selected for the final round i.e. closed camp. Out of them, one candidate dropped out at the last moment so 35 candidates were groomed in the 3 days closed camp at Mount Princess Hotel, Dhulikhel, Kavre. They were given session under different topics related to Rotary, Rotaract, Community, Career, Communication, Project Management and TEACH mission.
After the 3 days grooming, the participants were taken to Nepal Academy Hall on March 9, 2019 and they presented themselves at the event finale. They went through an introductory round from which top 10 male and female contestants were selected. In this round, they chose a judge and went through a Q & A round. Through this, Top 5 male and female were selected who were given a common question by the chief judge DG Rtn. Chintamani Bhattarai. They were given 4 minutes to prepare for the answer and 1 minute to present it. From this, the winners and runner ups were chosen.
Rtr. Salil Raj Shakya (RAC Bhaktapur) and Rtr. Sareesha Shrestha (RAC Kist Medical College) won the titles Mr. Rotaract and Ms. Rotaract along with the sub titles Mr. Personality and Ms. Personality. The first runner ups were Rtr. Ayush Suwal (RAC Patan South) and Rtr. Urja Newa (RAC Patan West) who also won Mr. Popular and Ms. Creative respectively. The second runner ups were Rtr. Abhay Lal Shrestha (RAC Balaju) and Rtr. Maya Thapa (RAC Sainbu Bhainsepati) who also won Ms. Intelligent. Rtr. Aashutosh Badal (RAC Kopundol) and Rtr. Aarju Gaihre (RAC Tripureshwor) won Mr. and Ms. Congeniality. Rtr. Rishab Maharjan (RAC Mahabouddha) won Mr. Creative. Rtr. Aashish Khadka (RAC Kist Medical College) won Mr. Intelligent. Also Rtr. Lumanti Manandhar won Ms. Popular.
The judges for the Final Event were PDG Rtn. Jaya RL Shah, Mrs. Raveena Desraj Shrestha, Ms. Evana Manandhar, Mrs. Prava Amatya, Mrs. Rajani Pradhan, Rapper Mr. Nirnaya Shrestha NSK.
