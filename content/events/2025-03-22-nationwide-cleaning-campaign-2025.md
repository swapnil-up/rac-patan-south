Stronger together! The Nationwide Cleaning Campaign 2025 at Banglamukhi Mandir was a success, thanks to the dedication of all the Rotaractors and supporters who showed up for a cleaner community. When we work together, we make a difference! The Nationwide Cleaning Campaign 2025 was not just about cleaning—it was about taking responsibility and inspiring change. Seeing so many passionate individuals come together for a cleaner, greener future was truly uplifting! Nationwide Cleaning Campaign 2025 
A cleaner tomorrow starts with us! On March 22, 2025, we’re joining hands at Banglamukhi Mandir to take a step toward a greener and healthier community. Organized by Rotaract District 3292 and hosted by incredible Rotaract Clubs, this campaign is a call to action for sustainability and collective responsibility. Let’s clean, protect, and preserve our surroundings—together!
