Fellowship and information sharing with Parent CLub

Celebrating World Rotaract Week, Rotaract Club of Patan South conducted a joint meeting with the parent Club, Rotary Club of Patan South on March 8, 2016 (Tuesday) at The Entrance Café, Pulchowk. The two clubs discussed about the upcoming joint event ie the Blood Donation Camp and Health Camp at Lamatar. As a Rotaract Club, the club is accountable to its parent club about the events and projects it has been doing so the club also presented a short presentation about its activities in Rota Year 2015-16. The members from both the clubs got to know each other and the club well.

- Duration: 5:30pm to 7pm
