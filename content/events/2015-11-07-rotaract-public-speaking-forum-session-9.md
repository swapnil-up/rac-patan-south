Develop public speaking skills of Rotaractors.

Rotaract Public Speaking Forum Session 9 was held on November 7 at Future Star High School. The session is held in the first Saturday of each month hosted by one of the four associated clubs. The session was divided into three sessions: the Prepared Speech session where Feature Speaker from each club came up and delivered their speech in the allotted time of 5 minutes. The session was the Table Topic session where Rotaractors gave impromptu speech from the topics provided to them. The third session was the evaluation session where both the Featured Speakers and Table Topic Speakers were evaluated on Grammar, Ahh counts and time. The Featured Speakers were further evaluated on the basis of eye contact, body posture, speech speed, delivery speed, etc. The session was followed by a brief presentation by IPDRR Rtr. Amit Bajracharya about the Ten Levels of Toastmasters Speech.

- Duration: 9am to 10:30am
