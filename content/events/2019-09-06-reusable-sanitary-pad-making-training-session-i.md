To spread awareness about plastic pollution caused due to store bought sanitary pads.

The project was facilitated by Rtr. Kripa Shakya. A group of 24 students participated and learned to make reusable sanitary pads. It awared them about the materials used in the store bought sanitary pads which is not healthy to the environment.

- Duration: 1 hr
- Rotaractors: 2; Visitors: 24
