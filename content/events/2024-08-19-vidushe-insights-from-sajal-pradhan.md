The second session of our project: Vidushe. In this session, we featured Sajal Pradhan, the Co-Founder of Best Paani. Best Paani creates non-electric, non-chemical, affordable rainwater harvesting systems using locally available materials to address water shortages in Nepali communities, particularly in government schools, and to tackle gender inequality in education access. Since the 2015 Nepal Earthquake, the organization has provided safe water access to 300,000 people across 20 earthquake-affected districts and recharged over 10 million liters of rainwater into the groundwater aquifer in Nepal. Miss Pradhan has received multiple awards for her work and is also a TED Speaker, where she shares her experiences in this field.
