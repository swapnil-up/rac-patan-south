To make students aware about the disposable pads which are loaded with nasty chemicals that might lead to adverse health effects

The training was facilitated by Rtr. Kripa Shakya and aimed to teach the students about the use of reusable sanitary pads. The students were divided into groups and were provided with materials(flannel, cotton canvas, a pair of scissors, needle & thread , buttons, chalk, patterns)to make reusable sanitary pad. All the students showed their interests.

- Duration: 1 hr
