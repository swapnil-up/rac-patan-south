- To teach the middle aged women about menstrual hygiene and the making of reusable cloth sanitary pad
- To help them become aware about the rise in plastic pollution, health problems created by store bought sanitary pads
- To help them know how it differs and is advantageous economically, environmentally and a healthy alternative

The project was solely initiated to help tackle plastic pollution as well as create a platform where both men and women learn freely about periods. It was also so that women face lesser health problems during periods, additionally it helps them save money on the long run.

- Duration: 1.5 hrs
- Visitors: 21
