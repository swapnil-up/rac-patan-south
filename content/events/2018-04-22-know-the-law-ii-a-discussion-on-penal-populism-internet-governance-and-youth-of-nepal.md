A discussion on Penal Populism, Internet Governance and Youth of Nepal

Know the Law II was conducted with discussion on Penal populism, Internet Governance and Youth of Nepal. Rtr. Dikchya Raut, Rtr. Anisha Thapa and Rtr. Bibek Kumar Thakur were the speakers. Rtr. Bibek Thakur presented on Penal Populism with examples reflecting complexities of the issue. Rtr. Anisha Thapa presented on cybercrime with focus of gender related issues. Rtr. Dikchya Raut introduced the participants to the concept of Internet Governance and had interactive discussion on internet governance.

- Duration: 2 hours
- Rotaractors: 3
