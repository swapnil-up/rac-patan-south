‘The Book Donation Project’ was conducted to meet the demand of books in Child Correction home, Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. Child Correction home is such type of reform home where the person below 18 years of age involved in delinquency are kept to reform them. Since it is least focused area by the state and the government this programme intends to facilitate the reformation of the children by providing the quality educational materials.

For the month of September ie. Rotary literacy month, Rotract Club of Patan South conducted ‘The Book Donation Project’ to meet the demand of books in Child correction home (Bal Sudhar Griha), Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. so after a month long collection of books from various sources the club donated 490 child friendly books to the Child correction home.

- Duration: 1 hrs
- Rotaractors: 2; Rotarians: 1
