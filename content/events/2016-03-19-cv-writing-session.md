To learn about correct CV Writing

On March 19, 2016 RAC Patan South and RAC Lalitpur conducted a CV Writing Session at KCM, Gwarko which was facilitated by PDRR Rtr. David Shakya. The two hour session encompassed the format of CV Writing, the ‘Do's and Don'ts' of CV, what employeers look for in a CV and the recent trends in CV Writing. The session was witnessed by 45 participants who are about to and go have already entered the job market. The end of the session was followed by certificate distribution and refreshments.

- Duration: 9 30am to 11:30 am
