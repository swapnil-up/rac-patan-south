RAC Patan South organized a talk show on clean energy by Biogas Support Program (BSP) Nepal on 8th September at Kamal Mani theatre, Rato Bangala School. Experts from BSP Nepal delivered the talk on rain water harvesting and biogas power plant. They discussed the scope and market of these clean energies in Nepal. Also presenting the model of rainwater harvesting, the experts enlightened the crowd on various types of biogas plants and the one most effective and feasible in Nepal’s context. They even answered queries of inquisitive minds. The program saw a respectable number of keen interacts and rotaracts from various clubs from all around the valley willing to broaden their knowledge on the topic. It was indeed a very informative talk program with enthusiastic youths showing their interest in discussing energy issues.
