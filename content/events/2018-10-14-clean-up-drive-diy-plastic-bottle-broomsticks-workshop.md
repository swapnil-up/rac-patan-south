To upcycle the used bottles and make broomstick out of it
To contribute towards clean up drive initiated by Rotaract Club of Saksham RID 3011
To clean up St. Xavier's School, Jawalakhel, Lalitpur.

Project Detail:
Plastic bottles were collected at the beginning and bamboo sticks were arranged.
The plastic bottles were cut creatively to take the form of brushes of broom. The bottleneck acted as the brush cap.
2-3 bottles were overlapped in order to make the brushes work conveniently.
In the end, bamboo stick was attached to the bottle which completed our DIY Plastic Bottle Broomstick.
The plastic bottle broomsticks were used to clean up the school surroundings
Project Outcomes:
4 plastic bottle broomsticks were prepared completely
The school surrounding was cleaned
Upcycling concept got developed among the club members.

- Duration: 3 Hours
- Rotaractors: 13; Visitors: 2
