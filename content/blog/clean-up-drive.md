Last weekend, RAC Patan South organized our first community clean-up drive in Patan Durbar Square area. Despite the early morning start, over 20 members showed up with gloves, bags, and plenty of enthusiasm.

**Highlights of the day:**
- Collected over 50 bags of waste
- Segregated recyclables from non-recyclables
- Planted 15 saplings along the roadside
- Engaged with local shopkeepers about waste management

The response from the local community was overwhelmingly positive. Many passersby stopped to thank our members and ask about Rotaract.

We're already planning our next service project. Stay tuned!
