We are excited to welcome you to the official website of the **Rotaract Club of Patan South**!

As a youth-led community service club, we bring together passionate individuals aged 18-30 who are committed to making a difference. Whether you're looking to serve your community, develop professional skills, or connect with like-minded young leaders, Rotaract offers a platform for growth and impact.

Stay tuned for updates on our upcoming events, service projects, and fellowship activities.

**Service Above Self** — that's our motto, and we live it every day.

Want to get involved? [Contact us](/join) or come to one of our meetings!
